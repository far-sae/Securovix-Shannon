## Your Task

For each candidate chain:

1. **Feasibility Assessment**: Can these vulnerabilities actually be chained in sequence?
   - Does exploiting Step 1 actually grant the access needed for Step 2?
   - Are there timing constraints or session limitations?
   - Would a real attacker attempt this chain?

2. **Impact Amplification**: Is the combined impact greater than individual findings?
   - What is the worst-case scenario if the full chain succeeds?
   - Does the chain achieve a higher-impact objective than any individual vuln?

3. **Score Adjustment**:
   - If a chain is infeasible, mark it as "INFEASIBLE: [reason]"
   - If a chain is feasible, rate it 1-10 for combined feasibility
   - If a chain is particularly dangerous, mark as "CRITICAL CHAIN"

4. **Execution Order**: Confirm or adjust the optimal execution order

5. **Missing Links**: Identify if there are intermediate steps needed that were not in the original chain

## Output

For each chain, provide your assessment. End with a ranked list of the top 3 most dangerous feasible chains.
