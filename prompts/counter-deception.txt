## Your Task

Analyze the fingerprint data and behavioral signals to determine if any endpoints are honeypots, canary tokens, or deception infrastructure.

### Assessment Criteria

1. **Response Timing**: Are response times unnaturally consistent (synthetic) or increasing (tarpit)?
2. **Header Analysis**: Do server headers match actual behavior? Any contradictions?
3. **Authentication**: Does the endpoint accept all credentials (honeypot signature)?
4. **Data Patterns**: Is the data realistic or obviously fake (sequential IDs, lorem ipsum)?
5. **Canary Tokens**: Are there AWS canary keys, tracking URLs, or canary DNS records?
6. **Behavioral Anomalies**: Does the endpoint behave differently from the rest of the application?

### Output

For each suspicious endpoint, provide:
- **Verdict**: DECOY (skip entirely), SUSPICIOUS (proceed with caution), or SAFE
- **Confidence**: 0-100%
- **Evidence**: What signals led to this conclusion
- **Risk**: What happens if we interact with a decoy (alerts defenders, triggers IR, etc.)
