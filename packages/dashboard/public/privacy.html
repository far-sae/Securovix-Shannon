Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy describes what data Securovix collects, why, and what we do with it. We follow the principle of data minimisation — we keep only what we need to run the Service.

AI keys are isolated by organization. Anthropic / OpenAI / Google / Zhipu keys are encrypted before storage, are never returned by our API, and are decrypted only inside the service that performs an authorized scan.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not run analytics trackers (no Google Analytics, no Segment, no Mixpanel).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use your scan data to advertise to you or others.
  • We do not use your scan data to train models.

3. Third-party providers

When you connect an LLM API key, Securovix sends scan inputs and prompts to that provider under their privacy policy:

  • Anthropic
  • OpenAI
  • Google
  • Zhipu AI

If you use Google Sign-In, you also agree to Google's terms.

4. Cookies

We use first-party HttpOnly cookies for the signed-in session, active organization, and short-lived OAuth/OIDC state. The session is HMAC-signed, uses SameSite=Lax, and expires after 30 days. We do not use advertising cookies.

5. Data retention

  • Account data — kept until you delete your account.
  • Scan artifacts — 90 days by default; an enterprise deployment may configure a different period.
  • Completed job records — 30 days by default.
  • Operational logs — 30 days by default; integration delivery history is 90 days.
  • Verification/reset tokens — expired records are removed after 7 days by default.
  • Session cookies — 30 days.
  • Code Scan source — removed from process memory within approximately 30 minutes after the run ends; not written to the database by Code Scan.
  • Personal Shield submissions — processed only for the current request and not persisted; audit records contain risk metadata but not the submitted message or link.

6. Your rights (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Export your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@securovix.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 (120,000 iterations, 16-byte salt). Sessions are HMAC-SHA256 signed. Organization credentials are encrypted at rest. Production deployments use HTTPS. Payment-card data is handled by Stripe when billing is enabled; Securovix does not store full card numbers.

8. International transfers

Our servers and Supabase database may be located outside your country. By using the Service you consent to your data being transferred and processed in those locations under appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses where required).

9. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes

If we materially change this policy we'll update the date above and notify signed-in users by email or in-app banner.
