Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 May 2026

This policy describes what data Securovix collects, why, and what we do with it. We follow the principle of data minimisation — we keep only what we need to run the Service.

✓ Your API keys never touch our server. Anthropic / OpenAI / Google / Zhipu keys are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage and sent directly from your browser to each provider. We have no copy. Same for your scan source code in Code Scan mode — it's POSTed straight to the LLM provider.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not run analytics trackers (no Google Analytics, no Segment, no Mixpanel).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not log the contents of your LLM prompts or responses on our server — those go directly between your browser and the LLM provider.
  • We do not use your scan data to train models.

3. Third-party providers

When you connect an LLM API key, your prompts and responses pass through that provider under their privacy policy:

  • Anthropic
  • OpenAI
  • Google
  • Zhipu AI

If you use Google Sign-In, you also agree to Google's terms.

4. Cookies

We use a single first-party HttpOnly cookie (shannon_session) to keep you signed in. It's HMAC-signed, set with SameSite=Lax, and expires after 30 days. We do not use third-party cookies.

5. Data retention

  • Account data — kept until you delete your account.
  • Scan history — kept until you delete the scan or your account.
  • Session cookies — 30 days.
  • Submitted source code — discarded after the scan completes; not retained.

6. Your rights (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Export your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@securovix.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 (120,000 iterations, 16-byte salt). Sessions are HMAC-SHA256 signed. Production deployments use HTTPS exclusively. We do not store payment information.

8. International transfers

Our servers and Supabase database may be located outside your country. By using the Service you consent to your data being transferred and processed in those locations under appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses where required).

9. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes

If we materially change this policy we'll update the date above and notify signed-in users by email or in-app banner.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@securovix.com.
