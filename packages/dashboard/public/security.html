Security

Last updated: 19 September 2026
Responsible disclosure: report suspected vulnerabilities privately to security@securovix.com. Include reproduction steps and impact. Do not access another customer's data, disrupt service, or publish an unremediated issue.

Security controls

  • Organization-scoped authorization is enforced on scans, findings, integrations, AI credentials, identity settings, and Defender data.
  • Passwords are hashed; sessions use HttpOnly, SameSite cookies; TOTP MFA and recovery codes are supported.
  • Customer AI, OIDC, SCIM, connector, and queued-scan credentials are encrypted at rest and are not returned by read APIs.
  • Authenticated scan credentials use encrypted, exact-origin-bound, five-minute, single-use grants; passwords and cookies are not placed in streaming URLs.
  • Active scans and Defender routes require target ownership verification. Sandbox jobs run on a separate runner in disposable, non-root, read-only, network-disabled containers.
  • Production services expose health/readiness checks, protected metrics, audit events, retention jobs, and revocable service credentials.
  • Personal Shield treats submitted messages and links as untrusted data: it does not navigate to links, execute content, call an AI model, or persist the submission.

Data and subprocessors

Production data is stored in Supabase. Railway hosts the application services. Stripe is used only when paid billing is enabled. Customer-selected AI providers receive the scan inputs needed for inference. See the Privacy Policy for retention and processing details.

Independent assurance

Framework mappings in reports are informational. They do not certify Securovix or the scanned customer. Securovix does not currently claim ISO 27001 certification, SOC 2 attestation, or PCI DSS compliance. Current independent test reports or attestations will be listed here only after completion and approval for publication.
